CINEMATIC CHARACTER ARTWORK

The 17 finished character studies were created for this project using the built-in image-generation tool, guided by the approved knight concept and the user's references. Full prompts are recorded in Character artwork prompts.json.

The supplied city artwork is used in the landing hero. The cinematic images are distinct from the interactive 3D prototypes.

ELDER OPTION ADDITION
An elderly man was generated with the built-in image generation tool using the original elderly woman artwork as a style and composition reference. The original woman artwork is preserved. Both appear as selectable elder options in the Community story. The male option is artwork only; the old 3D prototypes are unchanged. Exact generation prompt: Character artwork prompts.json, elder-man entry.
